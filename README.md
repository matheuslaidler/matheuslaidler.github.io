### 📚 O Projeto

Uma plataforma centralizada de conhecimento com foco em:

- **Segurança da Informação** — Conceitos, técnicas e boas práticas
- **CTF & Wargames** — Writeups e soluções de desafios de hacking
- **Programação** — Linguagens, frameworks e padrões
- **Computação Forense** — Investigação digital e análise
- **Bug Bounty** — Relatórios e metodologias de pentest
- **Tecnologia** — Artigos técnicos e tutoriais práticos

O conteúdo é resultado de estudos práticos, pesquisa acadêmica e experiência em segurança ofensiva. Cada artigo busca ser uma referência clara e acessível para entusiastas e profissionais da área.

### ⚠️ Aviso Legal

## en-us

### 📚 About

A centralized knowledge platform focused on:

- **Information Security** — Concepts, techniques and best practices
- **CTF & Wargames** — Writeups and hacking challenge solutions
- **Programming** — Languages, frameworks and patterns
- **Digital Forensics** — Investigation and analysis
- **Bug Bounty** — Reports and pentest methodologies
- **Technology** — Technical articles and practical tutorials

Content is based on practical studies, academic research and offensive security experience. Each article aims to be a clear and accessible reference for enthusiasts and professionals.

### ⚠️ Legal Notice

The information shared in this repository is **exclusively for academic and educational purposes**. Responsible use is essential. We are not responsible for any actions taken based on this content. We repudiate any illegal or unethical activity.

### 🛠️ Built With

**Jekyll** + **GitHub Pages**, with customized theme based on Chirpy featuring a tech color palette (cyan #73ffe8 + green #00ff88).

Comments powered by **Giscus** (GitHub Discussions) — integrated GitHub authentication.
The articles are the result of my studies in technology and hacking and the project is a junction of my past scientific dissemination projects: Way of Security and Road 2 Technology, with the addition of forensic study.
